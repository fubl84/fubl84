I build software that replaces detours.

I have been at Yamaha since 2013: first teaching piano, then running a school office, then training teachers. Today I am responsible for pedagogical training across 18 countries, roughly 1,800 teachers at some 600 schools. Three systems came out of that work.

---

#### What I have built

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **[Examination app](https://flamingbattenberg.de/case/yamaha-grade-exam)** | In use since 2024 | Desktop app carrying the entire examination day at Yamaha music schools. Seven languages, a second examiner joining live from another country, end-to-end encrypted, no central database of personal data. 350+ examinations a year. Wrap-up per candidate down from 15 minutes to 3. |
| **[Rehearsal booking](https://flamingbattenberg.de/case/rehearsal-booking)** | Live in Hamburg & Vienna | Booking, payment and access through a QR code on the wall. Two countries, separate pricing, rules and VAT in one instance. Zero phone calls left in the process. London is being adapted now. |
| **[Live workout platform](https://flamingbattenberg.de/case/live-workout-platform)** | In testing, before go-live | A WordPress plugin stack replaced by a platform of its own: live workouts, server-governed timing, server-side rendering, Stripe billing. Around five hours of post-production per video disappear with it. |
| **[Hangar](https://flamingbattenberg.de/case/hangar)** | In daily use, 1.0 before autumn 2026 | Native macOS app pulling eight tools into one working environment for AI-assisted development. 15 projects run through it. |

These live in private repositories. They hold customer and pupil data, so the code stays where it is. The case studies carry the architecture, the decisions and the numbers.
